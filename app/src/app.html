    Model card

    What Triage learned, and where it should not be trusted

    A logistic regression trained on 9,600 of the bank's April–September 2026 personal-loan decisions and tested on the other 2,400.

    Coefficients

    Scaled features · + pushes towards decline

    At the default 0.5 cut-off

    Test set

    At 0.5 the model would decline 225 applicants the bank approved. That is why Triage only auto-declines at a much higher confidence.

    How the lanes work

    Auto-decline only when the model's decline confidence is at or above the threshold and the applicant has a bureau score. Analyst review for everything else. Analyst only for new-to-credit applicants: the model is right only 56.7% of the time for them (81.2% for scored applicants), and at 0.90 half of those it would auto-decline were approved by the bank.

    Cleaning applied to every input

    • Monthly incomes above ₹6,00,000 are treated as annual salaries keyed into the monthly field and divided by 12.
    • A blank income is filled with the median for that employment type.
    • A blank credit score means no credit history. The applicant is flagged as new to credit, and the median score fills the gap only inside the model.

    Limitations

    • The model learned to copy past analyst decisions, not repayment. Any mistakes or bias in those decisions are built in. Link decisions to repayment outcomes before relying on it.
    • Trained on six months of one bank's simulated data. Re-test on newer applications before live use.
    • Coursework for Calibo AI Academy (Path 2, KLU). The model makes no lending decision about real people.
